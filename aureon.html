Aureon

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Package: com.staticlabs.aureon

Aureon is a market-research app published by Static Labs. It has no Static Labs user account, advertising SDK, brokerage connection, or real-money trading.

Information handled on your device

Aureon can store watchlists, paper positions, portfolio entries, research observations, alerts, settings, cached market results, and recent AI conversations in app-private storage. Static Labs does not receive this information. The optional Qwen model is downloaded to app-private storage and generates answers on-device.

Network connections

Aureon connects to third-party services to provide quotes, screeners, analyst information, exchange rates, news, web search, video pages, and the optional AI model download. Depending on the feature, requests may include stock symbols, company names, market filters, or a question you typed. Network providers receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address, device/browser information, and request time.

Providers used by the current app include TradingView, Yahoo Finance, Google News, Bing, ExchangeRate-API, OTC Markets links, Hugging Face, and the Yahoo Finance and Schwab Network viewing pages. Embedded viewing pages may set cookies or collect usage data under their own policies. Aureon does not control those pages.

AI questions

Qwen answer generation runs on your device. When Web is on, Aureon sends a search query derived from your question and recent topic to search and news providers so it can retrieve current sources. Do not enter personal, confidential, brokerage, or account information. Links and AI responses may be incomplete or wrong and are not financial advice.

Google Play purchases

Optional Aureon Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details; Aureon does not receive your full card number or bank details. The app receives purchase status, product identifiers, a purchase token and signed receipt to verify and restore access. A verified receipt is stored in app-private storage. Static Labs can access order and transaction information made available through Google Play Console for accounting, refunds and support. Google retains transaction records under its own policies; uninstalling Aureon does not delete those records. Purchase information is used to provide access and handle purchases, not for advertising.

Notifications

If you create an alert and grant notification permission, Aureon periodically checks selected market conditions and may show a local notification. Alert rules and results remain on your device, although the market symbols needed for a check are sent to quote or news providers.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you remove it in Aureon, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Exported backups remain wherever you saved or shared them. Static Labs has no account or server copy to delete.

Children

Aureon is not directed to children under 13. It is intended for general market research by adults.
