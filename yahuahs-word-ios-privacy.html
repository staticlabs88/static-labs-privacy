Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026. Publisher: Static Labs / Charles Nwonuala.

This free iPhone and iPad app is an offline scripture reader. We do not collect or transmit your diary entries, prayers, saved verses, search queries or reading preferences to our servers. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud AI or purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app stores reading preferences, progress, diary entries, topical prayer notes and saved verses locally in its WebKit data store. This information remains on the device unless you choose to export a backup. Device backups managed by Apple may also include app data according to your device settings. Removing the app may remove its local data; export a backup before deleting it.

Speech and clipboard

Read-aloud uses Apple’s speech synthesis voices for the passage or prayer you select. The app does not record microphone audio. Copy controls place the selected text on the system clipboard only when you tap Copy.

Backups

Import and export use the iOS document picker. You choose the file and destination, including any cloud storage provider enabled in Files. Exported JSON may contain your private notes: anyone with the file can read them. Imported backups are validated locally.

Offline content and external links

Scripture, dictionaries and fonts are bundled. A read-only server bound to the device’s loopback address serves bundled assets to the internal reader; it does not expose your diary or listen on the local network. Source and support links open your browser. The websites you visit have their own privacy policies.

Contact

For support, corrections, or privacy questions, contact us through Static Labs Help.
