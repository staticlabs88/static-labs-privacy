Forge Web Browser Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026. Published by Static Labs.

Browsing and storage

Forge Web Browser stores your browser settings, bookmarks, favorites, history, and completed downloads on your device. You can remove entries or clear the saved History list in the browser. Websites you visit receive ordinary web requests, which can include your IP address, browser information, and cookies. Their handling of this information is governed by their own privacy policies. Address-bar searches are sent to the search engine you choose, which is Static Labs by default.

Forge Chat

Regular chat generation and imported PDF excerpts are processed on your device after you download the optional Gemma model. The model download contacts Hugging Face. The chat interface loads avatar images from forge-si.ca. If you choose Search Web in Forge Chat, the search text is sent to Static Labs to return results. These requests expose ordinary network information to the services that receive them.

Optional reports and sharing

If you report a response, Forge sends the selected AI reply and the reason you choose to Static Labs so we can investigate the report. Reported replies may contain information from your conversation. Use the Share controls to send a file or link to an app or person you choose.

Contact

For questions about this policy or requests concerning information you sent to Static Labs, email support@forge-si.ca.
