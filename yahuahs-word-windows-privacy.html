Windows privacy policy

Static Labs · Updated October 14, 2026

Your local reading data

Reading preferences, bookmarks, reading-plan progress, diary entries and saved prayers are stored locally in your Windows user profile. Static Labs does not collect or receive this content. No app account, advertising or app analytics are included.

Optional Diary purchase

The entire Diary is unlocked by a one-time Microsoft Store purchase. Microsoft processes payment, account and license information under its own privacy terms. Static Labs does not receive your payment card details. Restore purchase checks your Microsoft Store entitlement. Bible reading and study remain free.

Offline content and listening

Bible, interlinear and dictionary content is bundled for offline reading. Listening uses installed Windows speech voices.

Backups and security

Exported backups may contain personal writing. You control where to save and share them. Protect these files and your Windows account. The app does not add password protection or encryption to diary records. Export important records before uninstalling or changing computers. Clearing the app's saved data removes locally stored records.

Microsoft services and external links

The interface uses Microsoft Edge WebView2. Windows and Microsoft Store may process installation and diagnostic data under Microsoft's privacy terms. External websites have their own privacy policies.
