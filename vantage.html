Vantage Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

About Vantage

Vantage is an Android awareness and news reader. It retrieves public weather, news, government and community feeds for the city and topics selected in the app. This policy describes information handled by the app and its service providers.

Information stored on your device

Vantage stores your chosen city and preferences, feed cache, saved story excerpts and (if an error occurs) a local crash report. These items remain on your device and are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. A crash report is not sent automatically. You may choose to share it yourself using Android's share sheet.

Requests to other services

When you use city search, the city name is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, which uses GeoNames data. Vantage sends the coordinates of your chosen city to Open-Meteo to obtain weather. To retrieve location-based news and disruption searches, it sends the selected city and country as a query to Google News RSS. For selected U.S. locations, it may send the chosen coordinates to the National Weather Service. The app requests public news, legislation, government, earthquake and community feeds from the publishers and agencies shown in Vantage. If you add Reddit communities, those requests are made to Reddit's public feed endpoint.

These services receive the request data needed to provide the requested response and may receive the network address and standard connection metadata associated with the request. Each service operates under its own privacy policy and terms. Vantage has no account system and no developer-operated server.

Information Vantage does not request

Vantage does not request GPS or background location permission, create an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or automatically upload saved items, preferences or crash reports.

Links and sharing

Opening an original article transfers you to that publisher or to your browser, which is governed by that service's policies. Sharing an article or crash report is optional and only happens after you choose a share destination.

Children

Vantage is a general news and information reader and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
