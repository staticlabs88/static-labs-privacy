Forge Chat Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

On-device features

Forge Chat does not require an account. The bundled model, voice playback, on-device speech recognition, and document text extraction process content on your device. Chats, preferences, and imported material are stored locally. You can delete your conversations in the app.

Optional connections

If you use the in-app browser, the websites you visit receive your requests and ordinary connection information. When you choose a page as a source, its readable text can be used in your reply. If you connect to an LM Studio or Ollama server, the app sends your chat context and relevant source excerpts to the server you configure. Those services are governed by their own settings and policies.

Support

Reporting a response opens an email draft for you to review. If you send it, we receive the contents and contact information you choose to include. Forge Chat does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Questions: support@forge-si.ca.
