Forge Chat Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

On-device features

Forge Chat does not require an account. The bundled model, voice playback, on-device speech recognition, image understanding, and document text extraction process content on your device. Chats, preferences, and imported material are stored locally. You can delete your conversations in the app.

Apple Health plugin

With your permission, Forge can read the activity, sleep, and body weight categories you choose. Forge reads and summarizes the categories you choose on your device. The dashboard keeps the retrieved summary in memory for the session. Questions and replies in the main conversation are saved with that conversation and may include information you discussed. You can delete conversations in Forge and revoke access in Apple Health. Forge does not use Apple Health data for advertising.

Optional connections

With the Web or Research plugin selected, Forge sends search terms to Static Labs Search or retrieves a requested web page. Follow-up searches can include the previous Web question for context. The search provider or website receives these requests and ordinary connection information. Research can request public statistical series from the World Bank using indicator and country or region codes. Retrieved excerpts and source links are supplied to your selected model for the answer. When you request source pictures, Research reads reachable public source pages and displays available images from those pages or their image hosts. Those sites receive ordinary web requests. If you switch to a remote model such as an OpenAI-compatible API, LM Studio or Ollama, Forge asks you to review what will be shared with the chosen server before sending the request. Information already in the conversation can be included. Those services are governed by their own settings and policies.

Custom data connections

When you choose Fetch, Forge sends a read-only request to the HTTPS data endpoint you configured. Credentials stay in device-only Keychain and are sent only to that service. Services requiring a query-key credential receive it in their HTTPS request; Forge does not log these credential-bearing request URLs. Redirects are refused. JSON responses are held in memory for this session. You review a labelled excerpt before using it in Finance or Research; analysis runs on this iPhone, and these data-context exchanges are excluded from saved chat history and later remote-model context. Changing conversations, clearing the source or backgrounding the app clears active response context. Keys and connection settings remain until you clear or delete them.

Optional IBKR Flex uses the token and Query ID you enter to retrieve reports directly from Interactive Brokers. Only selected financial fields are supplied to the local model, and account identifiers are masked. This is report data, not a live trading connection. No orders are placed. If you enable web research for a data source, each outgoing search question is shown for your review; account reports and API responses are not automatically included in those searches.

Bluetooth workouts

When you choose Find sensor, Forge requests Bluetooth access to discover nearby devices advertising the standard Heart Rate Profile. It connects only to the sensor you select. Received heart-rate readings and your workout stay on this iPhone. Recording pauses when Forge goes into the background. A workout and its received heart-rate samples are sent to Apple Health only when you review and choose Save and grant the requested Health permission. Workout explanations run on this iPhone. Discard the local workout in Forge or delete saved records in Apple Health. Readings during connection gaps are not inferred.

Plugins and API keys

Planner requests access to calendars or reminders when you use the corresponding action. Changes requested by AI require your confirmation. Fitness, Health, and Nutrition can summarize authorized dietary records alongside steps on your iPhone. Scan food processes the image on the device. Only after you review the amounts and choose Save to Apple Health does Forge request permission to write those dietary records. Vision processes camera scans, barcodes and selected photos on the phone. The Finance ledger uses protected local storage and can be deleted in Finance. Recent plugin activity records the action name, time and outcome.

Optional Forge Desktop inference sends approved conversation text to api.forgesi.ca. MCP tools use the HTTPS server you configure. Brave Search and USDA FoodData Central use the key you supply for that provider. Travel sends trip search terms to Static Labs Search. Education sends paper-search terms to Crossref and explains supplied metadata and abstracts. Opening Google Scholar sends the search terms you choose to Google. A provider receives the search you approve and usual connection information. Food barcode lookup sends the reviewed barcode to Open Food Facts. Camera images and Apple Health records are not part of food lookups. Credentials are stored in iOS Keychain. Keys for an OpenAI-compatible provider are scoped to its configured HTTPS API address. Clear a key in Settings or revoke it with its provider.

Forge Desktop and Forge Link

When you connect Forge Link, its embedded Tailscale networking service receives device and network identifiers, IP addresses, usage information and diagnostic data to establish and maintain the private connection. The networking service may use usage data for analytics. Forge Link sends your chosen tasks and messages to the paired PC and its configured providers. The PC controls file access and work permissions. Mobile chat history stays separate. Pairing credentials and the encryption key for the last Link conversation are stored in device-only Keychain. Disconnect and forget in Link clears the pairing and cached conversation. Files you export and history on the PC are managed separately. The sample project runs locally.

Link voice can remain active with the screen locked when you explicitly start background voice. Stop it in Link or from the lock screen. If you turn off the offline microphone option in Link, Apple may process audio for speech recognition. Forge Link privacy information.

Diary, journals and optional private iCloud sync

Dated entries, notebooks, tags and source links are saved on your iPhone. With your separate confirmation, Forge uses your private Apple CloudKit database to sync your library. You may additionally enable personal AI chats and attachments, or user prompts and display settings. API keys, desktop pairing credentials, transient Health/Finance data connections and People messages are excluded from this personal sync. Apple processes iCloud data under your Apple Account and settings. Turning sync off stops new updates but does not automatically erase existing iCloud copies. Exported copies and device backups are managed separately. The app records the accepted Terms version and time locally; this is not a payment authorization.

Optional People accounts and encrypted delivery

Private AI chat does not require an account. People uses a separate Cloudflare-hosted service for profiles, invitations, encrypted message and media delivery, and direct-call signaling. It does not grant access to the Forge business desktop accounts. Registration supplies a display name, username, public encryption/signing keys, a derived authentication credential, an encrypted identity backup and a recovery verifier. Original passwords, recovery keys and private message keys are not sent to the service. A username is not proof of legal identity. You may choose to save your login in Apple Passwords using Apple's confirmation.

The service processes your account identifier and profile, conversation membership, invitations, encrypted envelopes and encrypted photos/videos, delivery and read/mute state, encrypted call signaling, session/security records and the connection information needed to operate it. Messages and media are encrypted on the sending device for current members before upload. The service can see account and delivery metadata. Recipients can read and retain the content you send. Choosing a contact opens the system picker and invitation composer; Forge does not upload your device address book or match unverified phone numbers. In-app membership and invitation relationships form a social graph held by the service.

Encrypted delivery content and media are scheduled for removal after up to seven days. Expired sessions, invitations and call-signaling records are also cleaned up; operational cleanup may take time to run. History already received remains on recipients' devices. Account profiles, membership, encrypted identity backups, block records and submitted abuse reports remain while needed to provide and protect the service, until deletion or an applicable legal retention requirement. Delete your People account in People → Account to remove its profile and associated server records. This does not erase copies other members already received or your separate private AI chats.

If you enable notifications, Forge registers app-specific device tokens and the delivery environment with the People service and uses Apple Push Notification service. The alert contains a generic notice rather than message text or attachment content. Apple and Cloudflare process the delivery requests. You can mute a conversation or turn notifications off. Direct voice calls use encrypted WebRTC media; the service delivers encrypted setup messages and is not an audio-recording service. Calls may fail on restrictive networks because no paid relay is currently configured. No emergency-call functionality is provided.

Reports send the reason, account/conversation/message identifiers and only the selected readable message evidence you explicitly choose to include. The owner reviews this evidence for abuse handling; other encrypted messages and attachments are not automatically decrypted for a report. Report and Block controls are available in conversation details or a message's menu. Contact support@forge-si.ca for privacy, abuse or account assistance.

An @username invitation in private AI chat opens a review for a shared AI conversation. Earlier private chats, files, plugins and account access are not automatically shared. New shared messages are sent only to the current members. Ask Forge explicitly processes the shared text locally for a reply and sends that reply encrypted to the room. Private plugin sessions and personal AI history are not automatically included.

Support

Reporting a response opens an email draft for you to review. If you send it, we receive the contents and contact information you choose to include. Forge Chat does not use advertising trackers. Optional Forge Link networking diagnostics and usage are described above.

For product help, visit the Forge website or email support@forge-si.ca.
