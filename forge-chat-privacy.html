Forge Chat Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

On-device features

Forge Chat does not require an account. The bundled model, voice playback, on-device speech recognition, image understanding, and document text extraction process content on your device. Chats, preferences, and imported material are stored locally. You can delete your conversations in the app.

Apple Health plugin

With your permission, Forge can read the activity, sleep, and body weight categories you choose. Forge reads and summarizes the categories you choose on your device. The dashboard keeps the retrieved summary in memory for the session. Questions and replies in the main conversation are saved with that conversation and may include information you discussed. You can delete conversations in Forge and revoke access in Apple Health. Forge does not use Apple Health data for advertising.

Optional connections

With the Web plugin selected, Forge sends search terms to Static Labs Search or retrieves a requested web page. Follow-up searches can include the previous Web question for context. The search provider or website receives these requests and ordinary connection information. Retrieved excerpts and source links are supplied to your selected model for the answer. If you switch to a remote model such as LM Studio or Ollama, Forge asks you to review what will be shared with the chosen server before sending the request. Information already in the conversation can be included. Those services are governed by their own settings and policies.

Bluetooth workouts

When you choose Find sensor, Forge requests Bluetooth access to discover nearby devices advertising the standard Heart Rate Profile. It connects only to the sensor you select. Received heart-rate readings and your workout stay on this iPhone. Recording pauses when Forge goes into the background. A workout and its received heart-rate samples are sent to Apple Health only when you review and choose Save and grant the requested Health permission. Workout explanations run on this iPhone. Discard the local workout in Forge or delete saved records in Apple Health. Readings during connection gaps are not inferred.

Plugins and API keys

Planner requests access to calendars or reminders when you use the corresponding action. Changes requested by AI require your confirmation. Fitness, Health, and Nutrition can summarize authorized dietary records alongside steps on your iPhone. Scan food processes the image on the device. Only after you review the amounts and choose Save to Apple Health does Forge request permission to write those dietary records. Vision processes camera scans, barcodes and selected photos on the phone. The Finance ledger uses protected local storage and can be deleted in Finance. Recent plugin activity records the action name, time and outcome.

Optional Forge Desktop inference sends approved conversation text to api.forgesi.ca. MCP tools use the HTTPS server you configure. Brave Search and USDA FoodData Central use the key you supply for that provider. Travel sends trip search terms to Static Labs Search. Education sends paper-search terms to Crossref and explains supplied metadata and abstracts. Opening Google Scholar sends the search terms you choose to Google. A provider receives the search you approve and usual connection information. Food barcode lookup sends the reviewed barcode to Open Food Facts. Camera images and Apple Health records are not part of food lookups. Credentials are stored in iOS Keychain. Clear a key in Settings or revoke it with its provider.

Forge Desktop and Forge Link

When you connect Forge Link, its embedded Tailscale networking service receives device and network identifiers, IP addresses, usage information and diagnostic data to establish and maintain the private connection. The networking service may use usage data for analytics. Forge Link sends your chosen tasks and messages to the paired PC and its configured providers. The PC controls file access and work permissions. Mobile chat history stays separate. Pairing credentials and the encryption key for the last Link conversation are stored in device-only Keychain. Disconnect and forget in Link clears the pairing and cached conversation. Files you export and history on the PC are managed separately. The sample project runs locally.

Link voice can remain active with the screen locked when you explicitly start background voice. Stop it in Link or from the lock screen. If you turn off the offline microphone option in Link, Apple may process audio for speech recognition. Forge Link privacy information.

Support

Reporting a response opens an email draft for you to review. If you send it, we receive the contents and contact information you choose to include. Forge Chat does not use advertising trackers. Optional Forge Link networking diagnostics and usage are described above.

For product help, visit the Forge website or email support@forge-si.ca.
