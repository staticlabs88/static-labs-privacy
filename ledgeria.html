Ledgeria

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Package: com.staticlabs.ledgeria

Ledgeria is a local-first ledger app published by Static Labs. It has no Static Labs account, bank connection, advertising SDK, cloud-sync service, or automatic ledger upload.

Information handled on your device

Ledgeria can store transactions, balances, budgets, categories, notes, tax fields, receipt images, extracted receipt text, settings, backup-health times, and optional profile information. These records are encrypted in app-private storage. Static Labs does not receive them.

If you enable payment capture and grant Android notification access, Android gives Ledgeria broad access to notifications. Ledgeria filters for supported Google Wallet and Google Pay notifications, processes recognizable English payment alerts locally, and stores encrypted drafts containing an amount, currency, direction, source app, and time. It does not retain full notification messages, merchant names, or account details. Capture is optional and off by default.

Camera, files, biometrics, and sharing

You may take or select receipt images and PDFs. Ledgeria copies chosen receipts into encrypted local storage and uses Google ML Kit text recognition on-device. Biometric authentication, when enabled, is handled by Android. When you export or share a backup, CSV, JSON, or receipt, the destination app or storage provider you choose controls that copy.

Network connections and third parties

  • ExchangeRate-API: receives a standard network request, including your IP address, only when rates are refreshed. Ledgeria does not include transactions, receipts, balances, or profile data in this request.
  • Google ML Kit: Google documents collection of SDK diagnostics and usage metrics such as app/device information, installation identifiers, API events, and performance. Receipt images and recognized text are not sent to Google by the on-device text-recognition feature.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall Ledgeria. Pending payment drafts can be deleted in the app. Portable backups remain wherever you saved or shared them and must be deleted there separately. Static Labs has no server copy to delete.

Security

Ledgeria uses Android Keystore-backed encryption for local records and password-based encryption for portable backups. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your unlocked device and backup password.

Children

Ledgeria is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for general financial record keeping by adults.
