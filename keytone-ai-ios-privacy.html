KeyTone AI for iOS — Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026. Published by Static Labs.

Your typing and AI requests

KeyTone AI is an offline iPhone and iPad keyboard. The keyboard uses text you type through it, available surrounding text and selected text to provide suggestions, spelling corrections, tone rewrites, drafts, answers and AI chat. Gemma and the context predictor run on your device. The app does not send your typing, selected text, prompts or AI responses to Static Labs or a cloud AI provider. It includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Local storage and keyboard access

The keyboard stores settings and successful free-use counts locally. Free-use counts are per device and per AI feature, and may remain in the device Keychain after reinstalling. AI chat is temporary and is not sent to a server. The main app provides setup, Help, themes, a typing pad and purchase controls. App settings stay on device. Typing-pad text is held in the current app session; it is not uploaded or automatically saved as documents. The keyboard does not require Full Access. The app and its keyboard share only the local AI purchase entitlement through a private Keychain access group. The keyboard does not record microphone audio; system dictation, when used, is provided by Apple under its own privacy settings.

Clipboard and other apps

The keyboard reads copied text only when you explicitly choose a paste action. iOS controls the text context and selections made available to a custom keyboard. Some apps and protected text fields use Apple's keyboard instead.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. The main app uses StoreKit to retrieve prices, verify purchases and restore access. Static Labs does not receive your payment-card details. A verified local entitlement allows the keyboard to check whether AI access is active. Monthly access expires according to the verified subscription period; a permanent unlock provides access to the included AI features. Opening the main app refreshes purchase status, including refunds and revocations. Apple may provide the developer with store sales and financial reports under its own service policies. A purchase or restore requires an internet connection to Apple; keyboard inference remains on device.

Support and external links

Support is available at Static Labs Help. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Please do not include sensitive messages, passwords or payment details. Website links open external services with their own privacy practices.

Control and retention

Clear AI chat using its Clear chat control. Remove typing-pad text manually and change keyboard preferences in Settings. Removing the app deletes its ordinary app container; Keychain entries may persist. Contact support if you need assistance with locally retained access or free-use records. Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions in your Apple account; deleting an app does not cancel a subscription.

Changes

We update this policy when the app's practices change. The effective date identifies the current policy.
