Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026 · Android package: org.davar.interlinear

Overview

Static Labs provides Yahuah’s Word with Interlinear as an offline Bible reading and study app. No account is required. The app does not contain ads or analytics and does not request Android internet access.

Information on your device

Your diary entries, prayers you save or write, bookmarks, reading progress, searches and display preferences are processed locally. Static Labs does not collect, transmit, sell or share this information. Scripture and dictionary content are included with the app.

Backups, clipboard and sharing

You can choose to export or import a backup using Android’s file picker. You control where the file is stored and who can access it. A cloud storage provider you select handles the exported file under its own privacy policy. Copying content places it on your device clipboard. If you choose to share an exported file or copied text, the receiving service handles it under its own terms.

Retention and deletion

Local app information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Delete exported backups separately wherever you saved them. The app disables automatic Android cloud backup of its internal data; use the app’s export feature to keep a backup before uninstalling or changing devices.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Google Play and this website

Google Play may process installation and store information under Google’s privacy policy. This privacy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process routine web request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. These services are separate from the offline app.

Updates

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s privacy practices change.
