Forge Studio Intelligence Link privacy policy

Static Labs · Android, iPhone and iPad

Effective 13 October 2026

Your phone connects to your PC

Forge Studio Intelligence Link is an Android, iPhone and iPad companion to the Forge Studio Intelligence Windows application. It sends your messages, selected prompts, task instructions and requested files to the PC you pair with. That PC processes and stores your conversations and Work outputs. When you choose a local model, inference runs on that PC. The companion is not a standalone cloud AI service.

Information used by the app

Connection addresses, pairing credentials and device preferences establish and remember your connection. Messages, task instructions, attachments and generated files are transferred between your phone and paired PC to provide Chat and Work. Camera access is used to scan a pairing QR code, not for video calls. Requested downloads and exported files are saved on your device. Shared folders and Work capabilities depend on access you enable on the PC.

Speech recognition

Microphone audio is used when you enable voice input. On iPhone and iPad, the app uses Apple speech recognition. Offline microphone uses supported on-device recognition; when offline recognition is disabled, audio may be sent to Apple’s speech-recognition service. Android uses its configured device speech-recognition service, whose online modes may send audio to that provider. Recognized text is sent to your paired PC and the model you select. A local AI model does not make all networking or recognition features offline.

Online providers and private networking

When you choose a remote AI model, web search, image or video API, or an external tool, the PC sends the information needed for that request to your chosen provider. That provider’s privacy and retention terms apply. Built-in Tailscale networking processes account and connection information, device and network identifiers, and technical routing metadata to connect your devices. Network transport is encrypted. The app has no advertising SDK or marketing analytics SDK. Static Labs does not sell your conversations or microphone recordings. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send.

Voice and background operation

PC-generated voice audio is transferred to your phone for playback. Device voice uses the installed speech engine. An Android hands-free session uses a foreground service with a visible notification. On iPhone and iPad, background voice uses an active microphone/audio session and iOS background audio support. Capture remains active until you stop the session, an interruption or failure occurs, or the one-hour session deadline is reached. Recognition pauses while a reply plays. iOS shows its microphone indicator, and active background voice provides lock-screen playback/Stop controls. Stop the session or deny microphone permission to end recording. Animated portraits and the 3D avatar are app assets, not recordings of a real video-call participant.

Storage, retention and deletion

History and Work outputs remain on the paired PC until you delete them in Forge. Chat and Work have separate deletion controls; deleting a conversation does not automatically delete original project files. On iOS, pairing credentials are stored in the device Keychain, preferences in app-local settings, and the last-chat cache is encrypted with a device-only Keychain key. Use Disconnect & forget this phone to remove its pairing. Uninstalling removes the iOS app container, but Keychain entries and exported Files are separate; disconnect before uninstalling to reset pairing, and delete saved/exported files separately in Files. On Android, clear app storage or uninstall to remove local app data; downloaded files may remain until deleted. Support correspondence is retained as needed to address your request; contact us to request deletion. Remote providers control retention of data they receive.

Security, audience and choices

Pairing and encrypted transport protect the connection. Protect your PC, phone and provider accounts, and share folders only when needed. Store releases are intended for adults aged 18 and over and are not directed to children; displayed age ratings may vary by country or operating system. Do not send sensitive information to a provider or tool unless you understand its handling of that data. You can disable microphone and camera permissions in your device settings, choose supported offline recognition, select local PC models, stop background sessions, and disconnect pairing.

Changes and contact

Static Labs may update this policy as the app changes and will update its effective date. Privacy questions, support requests or deletion requests: support@forge-si.ca.
